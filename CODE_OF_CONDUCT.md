# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
trolling, and publishing another person's private information without
permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to security@cadenya.com. Project
maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.
